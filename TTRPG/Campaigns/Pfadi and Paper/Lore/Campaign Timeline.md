# Campaign Timeline & Chronology

This timeline tracks the chronological history of the [[Pfadi and Paper]] campaign world, integrating character origins, tragic events, and historical milestones leading into the campaign.

## Pre-Campaign Chronology

### Year 101
- **Birth of [[Freya]]:** [[Freya]] is born, later serving as a border guard for the elven kingdom of [[King of Aurelanor|Aurelanor]], while her father serves as advisor to the King.

### Year 383
- **Birth of [[Caspian Nyx]]:** Born into an impoverished family in a small river village with his mother, father, and two younger brothers.

### Age & Childhood Milestones (Caspian)
- **Age 8 (Year ~391):** [[Caspians Younger Brothers|Caspian's younger brother]] tragically drowns in the river after getting tangled in an underwater plant, causing their mother to withdraw emotionally.
- **Age 12 (Year ~395):** Caspian's father dies of illness just as Caspian attempts to steal medicine for him.
- **Age 15 (Year ~398):** Caspian befriends **[[Joffrey Rabenstein|Joffrey]]**, teaching him how to steal. During a major heist on a wealthy Rotenburg vault with his remaining younger brother, Joffrey betrays them when cornered by guards—stabbing Caspian, leaving him and his brother to be captured, and fleeing with the gold.
- **Captivity & Escape:** Caspian and his brother are enslaved and tortured. When his brother breaks his leg, he is fed alive to beasts before Caspian's eyes. Caspian escapes, returning to find his mother committed suicide and their home burned down. He begins life as a wandering rogue.

### The Fall of Aurelanor (Pre-Campaign)
- **Yuan-Ti Coastal Invasion:** Yuan-Ti raiders launch a devastating invasion overrunning [[King of Aurelanor|Aurelanor]].
- **Assassination:** In the royal hall, a massive 25-foot snake assassinates [[Freyas Father|Freya's father]]. His dying words command [[Freya]] to flee and eventually return to rebuild the kingdom.

### Recent Pre-Campaign Events
- **[[Sunny]]'s Banishing & Training:** After being banished from his temple, Sunny stays near the elven forests, sharpening his combat skills against local monsters through the adventurer's guild.
- **[[Akira]]'s Journey:** The elven wizard and sage [[Akira]] wanders out to observe how elves truly live, traveling to the elven forests where she meets [[Sunny]] and brings him along on her travels.
- **The Party Unites:** Akira and Sunny cross paths with the rest of the party (including [[Caspian Nyx]] and [[Freya]]) during their long journey.
