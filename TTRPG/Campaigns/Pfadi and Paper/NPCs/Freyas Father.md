# Freya's Father

## Background & History
He served as a trusted advisor to the King of [[King of Aurelanor|Aurelanor]]. When Yuan-Ti raiders launched a devastating coastal invasion, overrunning the kingdom, he was tragically killed in the royal hall by a massive 25-foot snake assassin. Before his death, he used his final words to command his daughter [[Freya]] to flee and eventually return to rebuild the kingdom.

## Role & Connections
- Father and mentor of [[Freya]]
- Advisor to the King of Aurelanor
- Slain during the Yuan-Ti invasion
