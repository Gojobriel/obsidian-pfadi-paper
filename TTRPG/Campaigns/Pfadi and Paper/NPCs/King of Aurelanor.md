# King of Aurelanor

## Background & History
The ruler of the elven kingdom of [[Freya|Freya's]] homeland, [[Freya]] served as a border guard and her father served as his trusted advisor. The kingdom was brutally overrun and destroyed during a devastating coastal invasion by Yuan-Ti raiders, culminating in an attack on the royal hall where a massive 25-foot snake assassinated the advisor.

## Role & Connections
- Sovereign of [[Freya]] and her father
- Victim of the Yuan-Ti coastal invasion of Aurelanor
