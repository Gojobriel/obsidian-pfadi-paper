# Caspian's Younger Brother

## Background & History
[[Caspian Nyx|Caspian's]] two younger brothers from their impoverished upbringing by a river. When Caspian was 8 years old, they went swimming in the river, and his brother tragically drowned after getting tangled in an underwater plant. This loss broke their mother emotionally.

*(Note: Caspian also had another younger brother who was brought along on the fateful Rotenburg heist, captured alongside him, and brutally fed alive to beasts after breaking his leg.)*

## Role & Connections
- Younger brother of [[Caspian Nyx]]
- Victim of the river drowning and subsequent tragic fate during captivity
